Built in code,
finished like photography.

Each Facet composition is a procedural render: three translucent glass sheets, lit by warm and cool keys, with a single sharp prismatic edge catching the light. Depth-of-field, halation, and fine grain are applied in the final pass so the output reads like a camera capture, not a generative stylesheet.

01 · Light

Stage the scene

Two lights: a warm key and a cool fill on opposite corners. The whole palette flows from this pair.

02 · Glass

Compose the panes

Three translucent sheets at different depths. Most go out-of-focus; one key edge stays sharp.

03 · Finish

Dispersion & grain

Rainbow fringes on the focal edge, soft halation around highlights, and a film-grain pass unify it.
