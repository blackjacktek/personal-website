[ The Approach ]

Built in code,
finished like photography.

Each Facet plate is a procedural render: glass sheets, warm + cool keys, a single sharp prismatic edge. Grain and halation are applied last, so the output reads like a camera capture — not a generative stylesheet.

01 · The idea

Quiet enough to live with.

A wallpaper shouldn't shout. Nine compositions built from one geometric language — quiet enough to hold the room, strange enough to notice.

02 · The rule

One palette. Nine variations.

Every plate is drawn from the same angles and the same light model. Constraint first, variation second. What comes out is a family — not a gallery.

03 · The pack

Three sizes. One download.

Delivered in 8K desktop, Retina tablet, and ProMotion mobile — 27 files total. Drops once, stays in your library. No subscriptions.
